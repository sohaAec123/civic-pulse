How CivicPulse Works

3-STEP PRIORITY FRAMEWORK
01

REPORT

Community members or officials submit detailed information about an unaddressed civic issue—ranging from contaminated drinking water to broken streetlights or ruined roadways.

Input: Title, Category & Impact Metrics
02

ANALYZE

The evaluation algorithm cross-examines multiple critical vectors: total citizens affected, severity degree, frequency of exposure, and whether a viable alternative service exists.

Engine: Weighted Priority Algorithm & Gemma LLM
03

PRIORITIZE

Receive an objective Priority Score (0–100), urgency level, structured key factors, AI rationale narrative, and recommended municipal intervention steps.

Output: Actionable Priority Dispatch

MISSION STATEMENT

About CivicPulse

When municipal resources and public budgets are constrained, every delay in addressing urgent community needs leads to compounded public hardship. Traditional grievance portals process requests on a first-come basis rather than objective urgency.

CivicPulse introduces a transparent, data-driven prioritization standard. By quantifying severity, population scale, exposure frequency, and alternative availability, CivicPulse empowers leaders to allocate resources where they save the most lives and mitigate the greatest harm first.
